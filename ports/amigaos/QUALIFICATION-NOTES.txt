GFOpenSoundCraft 0.1.0 native qualification build for AmigaOS 3.x
=================================================================

STATUS: INSTALLER-READY UAT2; NOT YET A RELEASE CANDIDATE. Native
GFOpenLayout rendering, bounded control events, SCF1 save/open, hostile-file
rejection, missing-synth degradation, synth-present message delivery, clean
exit, and Installer Pretend/fresh/update paths passed in an isolated 68040 lab
on 22 November 2026. Audible output remains unproved because the lab is muted.

Requirements
------------
- AmigaOS 3.x
- 68040 CPU
- gfopensynth.library version 1 or newer for audition; editing works without it

Included
--------
- native Amiga Hunk executable
- bounded session and MIDI sequence models
- GFOpenSynth reset, note-on, note-off and all-notes-off smoke path
- native GFOpenLayout editor with track, timeline and inspector panels
- working Play note, Stop and MIDI record-arm controls
- bounded SCF1 Save and Open at PROGDIR:SoundCraft.scf
- generated Installer and GlowIcons in the UAT2 LhA package

The Play note control sends a bounded C4 note-on/note-off pair immediately;
it is not timed transport playback. Add MIDI track is visibly marked as the
next slice and disabled. Audio clips, SMF, AHI recording/rendering and CAMD
device I/O are not claimed.
